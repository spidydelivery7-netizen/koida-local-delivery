Cancellation and Refund Policy

Koida Local Delivery aims to provide a smooth ordering and delivery experience. This policy explains how order cancellations and refunds are handled.

1. Order Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation as early as possible after placing an order. Cancellation may not be possible once the participating shop or restaurant has started preparing the order or once the order has been dispatched for delivery.

2. Cancellation by Koida Local Delivery

An order may be cancelled if an item is unavailable, the shop or restaurant cannot fulfil the order, the delivery location cannot be served, or due to other operational reasons.

3. Failed or Unsuccessful Payments

If an online payment fails or is cancelled before successful payment confirmation, the order will not be treated as a successfully paid order.

4. Refund Eligibility

A refund may be considered if payment has been successfully received but the order cannot be fulfilled, is cancelled by Koida Local Delivery, or another valid issue is confirmed after review.

5. Incorrect or Missing Items

Customers should contact Koida Local Delivery as soon as possible if they receive an incorrect item, a missing item, or face another significant issue with the delivered order.

6. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will normally be processed to the original payment method used for the transaction. The time taken for the refunded amount to appear may depend on the payment provider, bank or UPI service.

7. Delivery Charges

Whether delivery charges are refundable will depend on the reason for cancellation or refund and the stage at which the order was cancelled.

8. Contact Us

For cancellation or refund assistance, customers should contact Koida Local Delivery using the contact details available on our website and provide their order details and payment information where applicable.
