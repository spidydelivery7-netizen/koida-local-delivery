Pricing Policy

HUNKART provides customers access to products and food items offered by participating local shops and restaurants.

Product Prices

Product and food item prices are displayed on the website wherever pricing information is available. Prices may vary depending on the participating shop, restaurant, product size, quantity or selected variant.

Delivery Charges

Delivery charges may depend on the delivery distance and the delivery settings active at the time of the order.

From time to time, HUNKART may offer free delivery or promotional delivery charges. When such an offer is active, the applicable delivery charge will be shown during checkout before the customer makes payment.

Other Applicable Charges

Any additional applicable charge, if introduced in the future, will be clearly shown to the customer before the order is confirmed and before payment is made.

Final Payable Amount

The final payable amount shown at checkout includes the selected item cost and any applicable delivery or other charges. Customers can review the total amount before proceeding with online payment.

Price Changes

Prices may change based on updates from participating shops or restaurants. The price displayed at the time of checkout will be used for the order.
