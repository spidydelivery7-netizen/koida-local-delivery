Shipping & Delivery Policy

Koida Local Delivery provides local delivery services for food, grocery and other available products within our supported delivery areas in and around Koida.

Delivery Area

Delivery availability depends on the customer's location and the availability of shops, restaurants and delivery partners.

Delivery Time

Estimated delivery time may vary depending on the type of order, distance, shop or restaurant preparation time, traffic, weather conditions and delivery partner availability.

Food orders are generally delivered as quickly as possible. Grocery and other orders may require additional time.

Delivery Charges

Delivery charges may vary depending on delivery distance, order location and applicable offers. The applicable delivery charge will be shown to the customer before payment.

During a Free Delivery Offer, eligible orders may have a delivery charge of ₹0.

Incorrect Address

Customers are responsible for providing a correct delivery address and contact information. Delivery may be delayed or cancelled if incorrect or incomplete information is provided.

Contact Us

For any delivery-related assistance, customers can contact Koida Local Delivery through the contact details provided on our website.
