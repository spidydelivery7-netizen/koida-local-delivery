Privacy Policy

HUNKART respects the privacy of its customers and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

1. Information We Collect

When customers use our website or place an order, we may collect information such as name, mobile number, email address, delivery address, location details and order information.

2. How We Use Information

Customer information may be used to process orders, arrange deliveries, provide customer support, communicate order updates, improve our services and prevent misuse of the platform.

3. Location Information

With customer permission, location information may be used to calculate delivery distance and applicable delivery charges.

4. Payment Information

Online payments are processed through authorised third-party payment providers such as Razorpay. HUNKART does not store customers' UPI PINs, card PINs, CVV numbers or online banking passwords.

5. Sharing of Information

Information required to complete an order may be shared with relevant participating shops, restaurants, delivery personnel and service providers only as necessary for order fulfilment, payment processing or customer support.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect customer information from unauthorised access, misuse or disclosure. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website may use browser storage or similar technologies to remember customer preferences, saved addresses, account sessions and cart-related information to improve the ordering experience.

8. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services for hosting, authentication, database services, maps, payments or other functionality. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

9. Updates to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our services, technology or legal requirements change. Updated terms will be published on this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact HUNKART using the contact details provided on our website.
